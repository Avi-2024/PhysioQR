PhysioQR Doctor Referral and Rehabilitation Program Platform
1. Project Overview
PhysioQR will be a doctor referral-based rehabilitation platform.
PhysioQR agents will visit clinics and connect doctors with the referral program. After registration and approval, each doctor will receive a unique QR code and referral link.
The doctor will recommend the PhysioQR program to patients. When a patient scans the doctor’s QR code, the platform will automatically identify the referring doctor.
The patient will then:
Register on the platform.
Verify their mobile number.
Select their pain or health problem.
Complete a basic health assessment.
View the applicable rehabilitation program and price.
Make an online payment.
Receive access to day-wise exercise videos.
Complete exercises and track progress through the patient dashboard.
The platform will also calculate and manage doctor rehabilitation programme fee shares based on payments made by referred patients.
The system will have the following roles:
Admin
Agent
Doctor
Patient

2. Main Platform Objectives
The main objectives of PhysioQR are:
Register doctors through field agents.
Track which agent onboarded each doctor.
Generate a unique QR code for every approved doctor.
Track patients referred by each doctor.
Collect patient payments online.
Deliver day-wise rehabilitation exercise videos.
Allow different program prices for different doctors.
Calculate doctor rehabilitation programme fee share automatically.
Manage doctor withdrawal and payout requests.
Track patient progress.
Manage all programs, videos, doctors, agents, patients, payments and rehabilitation programme fee shares from one Admin Panel.
Generate detailed business and financial reports.

22.1 Split Model
Under the Split Model, the patient will pay the complete rehabilitation programme fee online through the doctor’s QR code. The verified payment will be divided between the doctor’s wallet and PhysioQR’s platform wallet according to the Admin-approved fee-share percentage.
App description: Split Model – The patient pays online. The total rehabilitation programme fee is shared between the doctor and PhysioQR, and the doctor’s fee share becomes available for monthly withdrawal.

Different doctors may have different total rehabilitation programme fees.
Example
Doctor A: ₹500 per patient
Doctor B: ₹700 per patient
Doctor C: ₹1,000 per patient
The Admin will also configure the doctor’s rehabilitation programme fee share percentage.
Example
Doctor A:
Total rehabilitation programme fee: ₹500
Doctor rehabilitation programme fee share: 60%
Doctor rehabilitation programme fee share: ₹300
PhysioQR platform share: ₹200
If five patients register and successfully pay ₹500 each:
Total patient payments: ₹2,500
Doctor rehabilitation programme fee share at 60%: ₹1,500
PhysioQR platform share: ₹1,000

22.2 Platform Fee Model
Under the Platform Fee Model, the doctor may collect their own clinic, professional or rehabilitation service fee directly from the patient.
The doctor’s separately collected clinic fee will not be processed through PhysioQR.
The patient will scan the doctor’s QR code, register on PhysioQR and pay only the Admin-defined platform fee online for access to the digital rehabilitation programme.
App description: Platform Fee Model – The doctor collects their own clinic fee separately. The patient pays only the platform fee online for access to the digital exercise programme.

The doctor does not receive a rehabilitation programme fee share from the online platform fee.
Example
The doctor charges the patient:
Doctor’s professional fee: ₹500
Collected manually by the doctor
The Admin configures:
PhysioQR platform fee: ₹200
The patient payment arrangement will be:
₹500 paid directly to the doctor
₹200 paid online through PhysioQR
Total patient expense: ₹700
PhysioQR will only record and process the ₹200 platform payment.
The manually collected ₹500 doctor fee will not be treated as PhysioQR revenue or doctor rehabilitation programme fee share.

47. Important Business Rules
The system will have only one administrative role called Admin.
A doctor must be approved by Admin before receiving an active QR code.
The patient must verify their mobile number before payment.
The patient referral must be connected with the doctor’s QR code.
The referring doctor must be locked after successful payment.
Program access must activate only after verified payment.
Rehabilitation Programme Fee Share must be created only for successful payments.
Rehabilitation Programme Fee Share must remain pending during the holding period.
A refunded payment must reverse the related rehabilitation programme fee share.
Doctors may withdraw only available rehabilitation programme fee share.
Doctor KYC and bank verification must be completed before withdrawal.
Every wallet credit or debit must create a ledger entry.
Previous orders must not change when the doctor’s price or rehabilitation programme fee share changes.
Doctors must only see their own referred patients.
Agents must only see doctors assigned to them.
Agents must not see confidential medical or financial details.
High-risk patient assessments must require manual review.
YouTube unlisted videos are not completely private.
All important financial and Admin actions must be recorded in audit logs.
